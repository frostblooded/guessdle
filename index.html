How to play

1. Mystery pet

Every day one pet is secret. Your job: find out which one. You get unlimited guesses! In this guide the secret pet is Ati.

2. Make a guess

Type a pet name and press SUBMIT. Every guess adds a row, colored by how close it is. Let us guess Cookie.
