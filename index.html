How to play

Guess the pet of the day. Each guess shows how close you are. Say the pet of the day is Ati:

Cookie
Black, Orange, White
Niki

Green: exact match. Red: no match. Cookie is not Ati, but has the same colors and owner.

Now say the pet of the day is Eddie:

Ati
Black, Orange, White
Niki

Orange: a list (colors, owners) where some, but not all, items match. Ati and Eddie share Orange and White. The photo is only a hint and is never colored.

A new pet appears every day.
